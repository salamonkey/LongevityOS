Button — the primary tap action. Use for form submits, sheet confirms, and prominent CTAs. Pill-shaped, one per view ideally.

<Button variant="primary" size="lg" iconLeft="plus" fullWidth>Impfung eintragen</Button>

Variants: `primary` (filled blue), `secondary` (soft blue), `ghost` (text-only), `danger` (red). Sizes `sm` / `md` / `lg`. `iconLeft`/`iconRight` take Lucide names.
